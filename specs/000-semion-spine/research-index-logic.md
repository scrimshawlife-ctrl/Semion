## OBSERVED

Degenerate / monstrative: letters on a diagram; Bunker Hill as “Here!”
No absolutely pure index (Baldwin Dictionary / CP 2.305).
Subjects of propositions: index, subindex (name, pronoun), or precept (recipe to obtain an index of an individual) — CP 2.330.
Common environment of two interlocutors can itself be an index (Atkins on *it rains*).
