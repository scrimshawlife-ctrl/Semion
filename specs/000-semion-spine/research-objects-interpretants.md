# Research — Objects and interpretants (SHADOW)

**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: REFERENCE. No extra packet fields this cycle.

## OBSERVED

Two objects (Welby / CP 8.343):
- Immediate: object as the sign represents it. Dependent on the sign.
- Dynamical / dynamoid / real: efficient object, not immediately present. Determines the immediate object. Reached by collateral experience.
Immediate object may be of a quite different nature from the dynamical (R339).

Three interpretants (names drift; this pair is the working one):
- Immediate: effect the sign is calculated to produce; meaning in CP 4.536; schema of what the type shares.
- Dynamical: actual effect produced in an act of interpretation; a single event; different each time.
- Final / normal / logical: effect that would be produced after sufficient development of thought.
Parallel vocabulary: emotional / energetic / logical. Scholars debate identity with the first trio. Do not merge in a packet.

## INFERRED

`object` on semion.frame.v0 is closest to immediate object. Null means immediate object NOT_COMPUTABLE, not “no fire existed.”
Dynamical object stays off-packet unless collateral provenance exists.
Typed `interpretant` (STATE_UPDATE) is a coarse dynamical interpretant. Not final. Not emotional.
Final interpretant must not become a meaning-score or Hub mind.
