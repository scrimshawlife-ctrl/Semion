# Research — Ogden–Richards triangle (SHADOW)

**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: REFERENCE. Not Peirce's triad. Not packet fields.

## OBSERVED

*The Meaning of Meaning* (1923), ch. 1 fig. p. 11. Vertices: Thought or Reference; Symbol; Referent.
Thought–Symbol and Thought–Referent: causal. Symbol–Referent: dotted, imputed only. “There!” is not on this figure.
Often mislabelled “Peircean triangle.” Merrell: three-way dyadicity, not genuine triadicity.
Richards: contextual theory of signs; proper-meaning superstition is a target of the book.

## INFERRED

Do not map Thought = interpretant, Symbol = representamen, Referent = object as identity.
The dotted base is a linguistic warning: words do not touch things by nature. Peirce's index *does* touch by Secondness.
