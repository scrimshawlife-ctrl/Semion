## OBSERVED

Tartu-Moscow (Lotman et al.): culture as correlated sign systems. Natural language is the primary modelling system. Myth, literature, ritual languages are secondary modelling systems built on it.
Semiosphere (Lotman 1984): the space in which semiosis can occur. Center / periphery / boundary. Boundary both separates and unites. Translation across the boundary is the engine of cultural change; inadequate translation is not a bug.
Programmatic 1973 theses: semiotics of culture studies the functional correlation of different sign systems.
Kull-style extraction of Lotman principles used in later work includes code plurality, non-translatability, autocommunication, semiosphere, semiotic inheritance, non-gradual evolution, boundaries, modelling.

Eco: codes correlate sign-vehicles with cultural units. Encyclopedia (not dictionary) is the model of competence. Unlimited semiosis is Peirce’s interpretant chain; Eco uses it and then limits drift via encyclopedia + community of interpretation.
Barthes myth is second-order signification on a Saussurean pair. Greimas square is already a TEACH chunk, not Semion gold.
