# Research — Ogden Basic English (SHADOW)

**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: REFERENCE. Not a Semion head. Closer to Hyperlex form economy and ingest paraphrase.

## OBSERVED

C.K. Ogden. Controlled English of 850 words presented 1930. Grew from *The Meaning of Meaning* (Ogden and Richards, 1923). Churchill promoted a form of it in 1943.
Split: ~600 things, ~150 qualities, ~100 operators/directives. Claim of ~16 operators replacing thousands of verbs by paraphrase (*enter* → *go into*).
Method: panoptic conjugation + elimination formula. Selection by work done, not raw frequency. Normal English word order kept. Subservient to Standard English idiom, not a sealed auxlang.
Roots: Bentham fictions/paraphrasis and panopticon metaphor; Welby significs and metaphor; Peirce via *Meaning of Meaning*.
