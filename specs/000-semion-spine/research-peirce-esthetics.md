## OBSERVED

Esthetics = science of ideals / of that which is objectively admirable without ulterior reason. Peirce: I am not well acquainted with this science; it ought to repose on phenomenology.
Prefers *to kalon* (fine, noble) over “beauty.” Antonym *to aischron*.
Esthetic goodness: ends that embody qualities of feeling; theory of deliberate formation of habits of feeling under self- and other-criticism.
Ethics and logic specialize that admirable: right conduct, true thought.
He is unsure even that the science exists, then builds the hierarchy anyway.
