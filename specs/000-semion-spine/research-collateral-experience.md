# Research — Collateral experience (SHADOW)

**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: REFERENCE. Not a harvest API.

## OBSERVED

The dynamical object is what the sign cannot express. It can only indicate it and leave the interpreter to find it by collateral experience (letter to James, 14 Mar 1909).
Terms: collateral observation / experience / acquaintance (R318; CP 8.178, 8.183; EP2 480–498).
Not Kantian Ding an sich: the dynamoid is representable in every respect, approached, never finished in one sign.
Dynamical object is not “outside mind” as a sealed thing; it is what is forced in perception including more than perception reveals (Welby).
