**Date**: 2026-09-11

**Primary**: CP 2.274 / EP2 272–273; CP 2.242; later semiosis definition

## OBSERVED

A representamen is First of a genuine triad; Object is Second; Interpretant is Third determined to stand in the *same* triad to that Object.
Genuine = not a complexus of dyads. Interpretant cannot be merely dyadic to the Object. The Third must be able to determine a Third of its own. Endless.
Sign (1903) = representamen whose interpretant is a cognition of a mind. Possibly representamens that are not signs.
Later: semiosis = cooperation of three subjects, not resolvable into actions between pairs.

## INFERRED

Ogden–Richards triangle is three dyads with a dotted base. Not this relation.
Saussure pair is two. Not this relation.
`semion.frame.v0` stores three poles plus class. It does not implement the endless Third.
Picca: packet interpretant is typed effect, not “cognition of a mind.” 1903 “Sign = mental interpretant” is the sentence we refuse as a product field.
