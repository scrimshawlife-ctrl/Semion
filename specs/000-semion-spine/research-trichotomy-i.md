# Research — Trichotomy I: qualisign / sinsign / legisign (SHADOW)

**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: REFERENCE. Head held. No classify path.
**Primary**: CP 2.244–246 / EP2 291; letter to Welby 12 Oct 1904

## OBSERVED

The sign in itself.
- Qualisign: a quality as sign. Cannot act until embodied; embodiment is not its character. No identity, only similarity. Also tone / potisign.
- Sinsign: an existent thing or event as sign (*sin* = once). Involves embodied qualisigns. Also token / actisign.
- Legisign: a law as sign. Type. Every conventional sign is a legisign; not conversely. Signifies through replicas, which are sinsigns of a peculiar kind. Also type / famisign.

Welby: *the* as one word = legisign; twenty *the*s on a page = sinsigns. `&`, *and*, and the spoken form can be one legisign.

Compossibility: qualisign can only be an icon (and a rheme). Symbol can only be a legisign.

## INFERRED

T0 atoms are almost always sinsigns or replicas. `sign_form` is the vehicle token.
`vehicle_class` held. Do not infer legisign from “the string looks like a word.”
