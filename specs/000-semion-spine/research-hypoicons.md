Manuscript variant used “examples” instead of “metaphor” for the third grade (R478).
Peirce: all necessary reasoning is diagrammatic. Existential graphs are icons of logical relations, not pictures of things.
Indices and symbols typically contain a hypoicon. Containment ≠ class.
