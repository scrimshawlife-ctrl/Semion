## OBSERVED

Victoria Welby. Significs named 1896 (“Sense, Meaning and Interpretation”), books 1903 *What Is Meaning?* and 1911 *Significs and Language*, Britannica 1911.
Triad: sense (organic / sensal response to environment), meaning (intention of the user), significance (far-reaching consequence, value, moral range).
Preferred significs over semiotics/semantics as less theory-laden and aimed at value in ordinary language.
Peirce reviewed the 1903 book, began correspondence, mapped her triad onto immediate / dynamical / final interpretant (letter 14 Mar 1909) and named the greatest discrepancy: his dynamical interpretant is actual effect; her meaning is intended effect.
Mother-sense vs father-reason: modalities of generating sense, not a sex lock.
Dutch significs movement later. Ogden/Russell mention in passing.
