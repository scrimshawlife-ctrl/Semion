## OBSERVED

Lotman: two communication models.
I–s/he — message and code stay put; addresser and addressee swap. Information quantity conserved.
I–I (autocommunication) — same carrier; a second code is introduced; the message is recoded and gains a new sense. Spatial gap replaced by a temporal gap. Qualitatively transforms information and can restructure the addresser’s code-set (*Universe of the Mind*, 1990).
Primary function: generate new information at individual and cultural levels, not transmit a constant payload.
