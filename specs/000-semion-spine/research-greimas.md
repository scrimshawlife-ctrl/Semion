**Date**: 2026-09-11

## OBSERVED

Greimas: elementary structure of meaning is oppositional. The semiotic square takes a contrary pair S1 / S2 and generates contradiction (~S1, ~S2) and implication. Not a Peircean triad.
Actantial model (1966): six positions, not characters — Subject / Object (quest), Sender / Receiver (communication), Helper / Opponent (power). Actors travel those positions.
Narrative program: an utterance of doing that changes a junction between subject and value-object. Propp functions sit underneath as a reduced quest grammar.
Isotopy: recurring semantic category that holds a discourse together.
TEACH already carries square / narrative-program chunks. Those are teaching rails, not Semion gold.

## INFERRED for Semion

Square and actants are **structure of a text**, closer to Athanor correspondence or a future BELIEF mapper than to `sign_class`.
Do not fold S1/~S1 into icon/index/symbol. Different theory, different packet.
A cultural slogan can still be a Peircean symbol and *also* sit on a square. Semion only emits the first.
Actant labels as optional `sign_content` tags on a culture_text row: allowed as provenance strings. Not a second classifier.
