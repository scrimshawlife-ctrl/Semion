**Date**: 2026-09-11
**Status**: REFERENCE. No extra classify head.

## OBSERVED

Symbol refers to its object by a law / habit / association of general ideas (CP 2.249). It is itself a general, hence a legisign.
Every conventional sign is a legisign; not every legisign is a symbol (demonstratives are legisigns and indices).
Replica: the token. Without the law the replica is mute.
Ten-class symbol slots: rhematic symbol (common noun), dicent symbol (proposition), argument. All symbols in 1903 are legisigns. Arguments can only be symbols.
