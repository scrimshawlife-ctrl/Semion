## OBSERVED

Three normative sciences, in dependence order:
- Esthetics: what is admirable in itself with no ulterior reason. Home of the summum bonum question.
- Ethics: theory of self-controlled, deliberate conduct. Asks to what end all effort shall be directed. Appeals to esthetics for the ultimate end.
- Logic / normative semeiotic: theory of self-controlled thought. Appeals to ethics. The logically good is a species of the morally good.

Ought has no meaning except relative to an end (CP 5.594).
Candidate ultimate end: concrete reasonableness — ongoing self-correction away from error, not a finished bliss-state.
