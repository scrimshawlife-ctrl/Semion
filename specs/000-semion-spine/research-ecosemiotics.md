## OBSERVED

Ecosemiotics studies sign processes responsible for ecological phenomena and culture–environment modelling (Maran and Kull 2014; Nöth 1998; Kull 1998).
Two historical poles: Nöth organism–environment (biological) and Kull culture–nature (cultural).
Roots: Uexküll Umwelt, biosemiotics, Lotman, Tartu.
Working concepts named in secondary literature: affordance, eco-field, consortium, ecological code, semiocide, biophony.
Maran (environmental signs) types signs by object/representamen access — embodied vs detached — without requiring a human interpreter for every row.
